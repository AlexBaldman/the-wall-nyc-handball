NYC One-Wall Handball

A playable rally prototype focused on feel first: serve flow, front-wall rules, first-bounce faults, spin, and a scrappy practice opponent.

Controls

  • Move: `WASD` or arrow keys
  • Swing: `Space`
  • Shot select: `1` flat, `2` lob, `3` kill
  • Spin: hold `Z` for left english, `X` for right english
  • Power: hold `Shift` while swinging
  • Serve / Reset: `R` / `Backspace`

Prototype Rules

  • Serves must hit the front wall first.
  • The first serve bounce must land beyond the short line and before the back line.
  • During rallies, returns must reach the front wall before the second bounce.
  • Miss the front wall, hit out, or let it die twice and you give up the point.
  • First side to 11 wins the match.

Build Intent

This extracted project is tuned as a feel-first prototype, not a full simulation. The goal is to validate pacing, spacing, and shot expression before bigger production work.
